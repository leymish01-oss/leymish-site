Questions

Is this product video maker really free?

Yes. Making and downloading videos is free: every template, the 4 free themes, your own colours, music, captions, the AI voice and recording your own voice. There's no sign-up and no watermark. Optional Designer themes are US$3 each, or US$5 for all 8 (one-time).

Is there a watermark?

No. There's an optional end card that says "Made free at leymish.com", and you can switch it off before you export.

Can I make a Black Friday video for free?

Yes. Pick the Black Friday sale template, type your discount, what it covers, your code and the deadline, and export a 9:16 or 16:9 MP4. There's also a Christmas gift guide template with three products.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos, your logo, your recording and the finished video stay on your device. The page draws and encodes the video in your browser.

Which browsers work?

Chrome and Edge on a computer or an Android phone. Safari 26 and Firefox on a computer may work. Firefox on Android can preview but not export.

Is the voice a real person?

No. It's an AI-generated voice (Kokoro) that runs on your device and is labelled as AI. There's no voice cloning. You can record your own voice instead, or use no voice.

What are Designer themes?

Eight extra looks, including Holiday Glow and Luxury Noir, plus Match my logo, which builds a theme from your logo's colours. Preview them free; unlocking one lets you export with it on this browser.
