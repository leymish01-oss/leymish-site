    Questions

    What's in the holiday gift guide video template?

    An opening line, three gifts each with a name, price and photo, a deadline or call to action such as your last order date for Christmas delivery, and your web address or @handle. You can change every line.

    Is the Christmas product video maker free?

    Yes. The gift guide template, the 4 free themes, your own colours, captions, music and the voice options are free, with no sign-up and no watermark. Optional Motion Packs, including Holiday Glow, are US$3 each or US$5 for all 8, one-time. The money keeps the tool free.

    Can I make a video for just one Christmas product?

    Yes. Use the Product promo template for one product, its price, up to 3 photos and three reasons to buy. On Shopify and WooCommerce product pages, "Start from your link" can fill the name, price and photos for you; some sites block automatic visits.

    Where can I post it?

    Export 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, or 16:9 for YouTube. Both are 1080p MP4 files you can also add to your product pages.

    Are my gift photos uploaded?

    No. Your photos, logo, recording and the finished video stay on your device. The page draws and encodes the video in your browser.
