What it checks

  • Was/now and strike-through prices, RRP, "save $X" and "compare at", when nothing says when the higher price applied
  • Countdown timers and urgency: "ends tonight", "today only", "hurry", "6 hours left"
  • "Up to X% off" when it doesn't say how many items get the top discount
  • Sitewide or "everything" headlines with exclusions, an asterisk or "conditions apply"
  • "Free" offers whose minimum spend or condition sits away from the claim
  • Added fees: booking, service or card fees and surcharges added on top of the price
  • "Lowest", "best" or "cheapest" price claims that need evidence
  • Limited stock claims: "only 3 left", "selling fast", "while stocks last"

Questions

Why check Black Friday sale claims at all?

The ACCC checks Black Friday and Boxing Day sales every year. After its 2025 Black Friday sweep it wrote to major retailers about countdown timers that didn't match the sale, storewide claims with exclusions, fine print that undercut the headline, "up to" discounts few items got, and was/now prices. Misleading pricing is one of its compliance and enforcement priorities for 2026–27.

When is a "was" price OK?

When it's a price you actually charged for a reasonable period just before the sale. If an item has been "on sale" for a long time, the sale price has become the normal price. Keep records of what you charged and when, because the ACCC can ask you to back up a was/now claim.

Does a flag mean my ad breaks the law?

No. A flag means the wording matches a kind of claim the ACCC has warned about, so it's worth a second look. Many flagged claims are fine if they're true and you can show it. The checker reads words only, so it can't know your price history or stock levels.

Can I still add a card surcharge?

Not on eftpos, Mastercard or Visa. Card surcharges on those networks have been banned in Australia since 1 October 2026. Other unavoidable fees, like a booking fee, need to be included in the price you advertise.

Is what I paste saved or sent anywhere?

No. The check runs in your browser on this page. The text you paste isn't uploaded or stored.
