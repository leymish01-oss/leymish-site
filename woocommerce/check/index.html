Can AI shopping assistants read your WooCommerce store?

Enter your store's address. In about 10 seconds you get 5 outside checks, built on the method from our census of 100 WooCommerce stores, with a score out of 5 and what to fix.

We only look at public pages, at most 5 of them, as LeyMishCheck, and we follow your robots.txt. What we keep.

What the 5 checks look at

  • robots.txt: whether the crawlers behind AI search and shopping answers (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Googlebot) may read your pages. Blocking training-only bots such as GPTBot is your choice and doesn't count against you.
  • Product data: whether a product page carries structured product details with a barcode (GTIN) or part number (MPN) and a brand.
  • Store API: whether WooCommerce's public product feed answers with clean data (no byte-order mark in front).
  • llms.txt: whether your store has a short file telling AI tools what's on the site.
  • Open for visitors: whether the store is in "coming soon" mode, which hides every product.

The free plugin runs 10 checks from inside WordPress, including every product rather than one, and tells you the fix for each in order of impact.

What we keep

Only a one-way hash of the domain, the time and the score, so we can count how many checks run. We keep the full result for 6 hours so a shared link loads fast, then delete it. We don't keep your address, your page content or your IP address (rate limits use a one-way hash that's dropped after an hour).
