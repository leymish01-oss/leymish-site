Your first 15 minutes

Everything here runs on your own site and stays free.

  1. Install the plugin

    2 min

    Plugins → Add New → search LeyMish AI Shopping Readiness → Install → Activate

  2. Run your first audit

    1 min

    LeyMish → Overview → Run the audit. It checks a few of your own pages the way AI shopping agents read them.

  3. Read your score and "Your next 3 wins"

    1 min

    A score out of 100 and a grade. Each win shows the points it adds and a Fix it button.

  4. Fix product gaps

    5 min

    LeyMish → Products. Use the one-click fixes: Set brand for all; Use each SKU as the MPN (only if you make the products); GTINs from a supplier CSV. Open See it the way AI sees it: red fields turn green as you fix them.

  5. Switch on feeds and schema

    3 min

    LeyMish → Feeds: tick the OpenAI and Google feeds, llms.txt, the UCP profile and product schema. Set the return policy to match your real one, then Save.

  6. Run the audit again

    1 min

    LeyMish → Overview → Run the audit again. "What we fixed" shows your score change.

  7. Turn on the weekly email

    30 sec

    LeyMish → Plan → Weekly email. Your site re-checks itself every week and emails the score and any alerts.

When something looks wrong

Quick answers

The audit says "partial result"

Some of your pages answered slowly. Run the audit again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, check that a caching or security plugin isn't delaying your own site's requests.

"AI bots are blocked by a firewall, CDN or security plugin"

Look for "block AI crawlers" or bot-fight settings in your security plugin or CDN (for example Cloudflare). Allow ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude search crawlers if you want to appear in their answers.

Where do I find a GTIN (barcode)?

On your supplier's product file, or the barcode on the packaging. If you make your own products, buy barcodes from GS1. Never make one up: the plugin rejects numbers that fail the check digit.

Pro says my licence isn't active

Open LeyMish → Plan and paste the key from your receipt email. If it still fails, reply to the receipt email and we'll sort it out.

AI visibility shows 0 of 10 cited. What now?

That's the starting point for most small stores. Fix everything in Products and Feeds, publish helpful pages that answer your shoppers' questions, and watch the trend week by week.
