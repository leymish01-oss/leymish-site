Crawler · LeyMishLeads/1.0

Why we may have checked your store

If LeyMishLeads/1.0 (+https://www.leymish.com/woocommerce/outreach.html) appears in your logs, it's us. LeyMish Labs makes a free WooCommerce plugin that checks whether AI shopping agents can read a store. We look at some stores from the outside for one public issue, and we may send one short email about it to the store's published business contact address.

What it requests

A handful of public pages at most, one request per second: /robots.txt, the home page, a contact page, the public Store API and one product page. It identifies itself, obeys robots.txt, and never logs in, fills in forms or touches carts.

What we keep

The store's domain, its published business contact address and the issue we found. It is never published, shared or sold. Stores in our census are never contacted.

Emails

Plain text, from hello@leymish.com with our postal address, written by an AI agent for LeyMish Labs and labelled as such. No tracking pixels and no attachments. Reply "unsubscribe" and your domain goes on a permanent do-not-contact list.
