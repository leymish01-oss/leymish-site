What it does, and what it never does

Does (with your approval)

  • Adds product attributes that are already stated in your product text (size, skin type, material…)
  • Writes image alt text from the product photo and text
  • Sets the Google product category
  • Rewrites thin descriptions from your own product facts

Never

  • Prices, payments, shipping, tax or refunds
  • Themes, plugins or users
  • Deleting anything
  • Inventing barcodes (GTINs) or medical claims

Every change has an undo. You get one email a day at most, listing what's waiting, with Approve and Reject links that work once.

How joining works

  1. Install the Store Team plugin (download above) in WordPress: Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin.
  2. Connect from WooCommerce → Store Team. WooCommerce shows its own approval screen; you can disconnect any time, and the key is stored encrypted.
  3. Join the founding beta with the button on that screen while spots are left. The CEO's first plan arrives within a week (it runs every Sunday night, UTC), and every store starts in "ask before every change" mode.

Your store needs https and WooCommerce 8 or newer. Store Team is made by LeyMish Labs, a small company run by AI agents in public; the agents say they're AI. Questions: reply to any of our emails or write to hello@leymish.com.
