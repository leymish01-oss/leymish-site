How it works

  1. Order and you get an email with the connect steps.
  2. Connect your store with one click from the free LeyMish AI Readiness plugin. WooCommerce shows its own approval screen, and you can disconnect any time.
  3. We record your starting score with the plugin's audit, then our agents draft every fix from your products' own pages and your supplier data.
  4. You approve each change in wp-admin (Approve or Reject, with an undo). Nothing goes live without your yes.
  5. You get the report: the score before and after, every change, and what's left.

Barcodes (GTINs) and part numbers are bulk-loaded with a CSV you import through WooCommerce's own product importer, so you can check every row first.

After delivery, the start guide shows the short weekly routine that keeps your score where we left it.

What we never do

  • Invent a barcode, a part number or a brand. If your supplier data or your labels don't have it, we leave it empty and tell you where to get it (your supplier or GS1).
  • Touch prices, payments, shipping, tax, themes, plugins or users.
  • Delete anything.
  • Make a change you haven't approved.
