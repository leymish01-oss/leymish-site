Refer a store, get 20% of the sale as credit

If you bought Pro or Pro AI and know another WooCommerce store owner it would help, send them your link. When they buy, you get 20% of what they paid as a discount code for any LeyMish product.

Get your link

Paste the licence key from your Gumroad receipt. We check it with Gumroad, then show your link and your credit. We don't keep the key, only a code tied to a one-way hash of the email on your purchase.

The terms, in full

  • What counts: a purchase of Pro or a Pro AI plan made from your link (it adds ?ref= and your code; the code is carried to Gumroad's checkout).
  • How much: 20% of the price the buyer paid on that purchase, after any discount and before Gumroad's fee. For a membership, the first payment only, not renewals.
  • When it's yours: once the sale shows up in our books from Gumroad, usually within a day. Until then it shows as pending.
  • Refunds: if the buyer is refunded or disputes the charge, that credit is cancelled.
  • Not your own purchase: buying through your own link doesn't count.
  • How you use it: from $5 of confirmed credit, ask for it here and we email a single-use Gumroad discount code for that amount within 2 business days. Credit is a discount, never cash, and it doesn't expire while we sell the products.
  • No spam: share it with people you know or in places where that's welcome. If a link is spread by spam, its credit is cancelled.
  • Changes: if these terms change, the change applies only to sales after the date we post it here. Last changed 7 October 2026.
