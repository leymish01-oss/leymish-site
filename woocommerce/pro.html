About the plans

What is a fix?

One suggestion for one product: its attributes, a clearer description, a Google product category, or image alt text. You see it next to what's there now and choose what to keep; nothing changes until you click Apply.

What is sent to the AI?

Only the product you click: its name, descriptions, attributes, category names and, for alt text, its main image URL. No customer, order or personal data, ever. We keep counters, not your product text, and the AI provider doesn't train on it.

Will it make up GTINs or specs?

No. It only uses facts in your own product text or visible in the image, never generates GTINs, EANs, UPCs or MPNs, and drops any number that looks like one.

How do I cancel?

From your Gumroad library, any time. You keep access until the period you paid for ends. The free plugin keeps working, and so does a one-time Pro licence if you bought one.

Is Pro included in Pro AI?

Yes. A Pro AI or Agency key unlocks every Pro tool while your membership is active, so you only need one key.

What Pro adds

  • Bulk editor for GTIN, brand and MPN. One table for all products, variations included. GTINs are checked (8, 12, 13 or 14 digits with a valid check digit) before saving, and go into WooCommerce's own GTIN field and WooCommerce Brands.
  • OpenAI product feed (JSONL) and Google Merchant Center feed (TSV) at stable URLs on your own domain, one row per variation, rebuilt a minute after you save a product. They follow OpenAI's Product Feed Spec and Google's product data spec.
  • llms.txt generator: your store summary, categories, policies and feed links at /llms.txt.
  • Weekly re-audit email to the site admin with this week's score, last week's, and the top three fixes.
  • Score history, so you can see the trend.

Private by design

Everything runs on your site. The only outside request Pro makes is to Gumroad, with your license key, to check it (when you activate, then once a week). Submitting feeds to OpenAI or Google still needs your own merchant accounts there.

You need

WordPress 6.4+, WooCommerce 8.0+, PHP 7.4+, and the free LeyMish AI Shopping Readiness plugin.

The honest part

No plugin can promise that ChatGPT or Google will show your products; they decide. Pro fixes the data and access problems that commonly keep products out.
