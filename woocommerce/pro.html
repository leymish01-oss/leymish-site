About the plans

What's free?

Everything that runs on your store: the audit, the Products tab and its one-click fixes, OpenAI and Google feeds, llms.txt, the UCP profile, product schema, the weekly re-audit and the report. Nothing on your site is locked.

What does Pro add?

Services that run on our side: weekly AI visibility checks, 500 AI fixes a month, outside monitoring of your store and feeds, and Store Team agents doing the weekly plan's work, each change waiting for your approval.

How fast is the upgrade?

About a minute. Click Upgrade in the plugin's Plan tab, pay at Gumroad, and the plugin switches Pro on by itself. Pasting the key from your receipt works too.

What is sent to the AI?

Only what you choose: for an AI fix, the product you click; for AI visibility, your questions and your store's address. No customer, order or personal data, ever.

Will it make up GTINs or specs?

No. It only uses facts from your own product pages, never generates GTINs, EANs, UPCs or MPNs, and drops any number that looks like one.

I bought Pro for $29. What now?

Your key keeps working: enter it in the Plan tab of LeyMish AI Readiness 2.0 and Pro switches on.

How do I cancel?

From your Gumroad library, any time. You keep Pro until the period you paid for ends, and the free plugin keeps working.

What Pro adds

  • AI visibility. Your shoppers' own questions (drafted from your categories, edited by you) are asked to a search-grounded AI model every week. You see whether the answer cites your store, which sites it cites instead, and the trend over time, with "what to change" linked to the fixes in the plugin.
  • AI fixes, 500 a month. Attributes, image alt text, a Google category and a clearer description, drafted only from the product's own page. You see Now and Proposed side by side; nothing is saved until you approve.
  • Outside monitoring. Every week we fetch your store, your feeds and your UCP profile from outside, the way OpenAI and Google do, and alert you when a firewall or CDN blocks AI crawlers or a feed breaks. A store can't see those blocks from inside.
  • Store Team agents. A CEO agent plans the week for free; on Pro, the Catalog and Reporting agents do the work, and every change waits in the Approvals inbox in your admin.
  • Before/after report with your AI visibility results added, to keep or send to a client.

Upgrade in a minute

In the plugin, open LeyMish → Plan and click Upgrade: $12/month. Checkout opens in a new tab; when you've paid, the plugin switches Pro on by itself. Pasting the key from your receipt works too.

The start guide shows the weekly Pro routine: check AI visibility, approve or edit the AI fixes, re-run the audit.

Private by design

The plugin sends nothing until you use a LeyMish service, and each service says what it sends before you use it: your questions for AI visibility, the product you click for an AI fix. Never customer or order data. The full list is in the plugin's readme under "External services".

The honest part

No one can promise that ChatGPT or Google will show your products; they decide. LeyMish fixes the data and access problems that commonly keep products out, and AI visibility shows you, with real answers, whether it's working.
