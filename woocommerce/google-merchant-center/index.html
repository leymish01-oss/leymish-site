What waiting costs

While an account is suspended, Google says its products are "disabled from appearing across Google", and disapproved products "stop showing across Google" until they're fixed (Google: Issues in Merchant Center). Asking for a review too early has a cost too: after a limited number of attempts Google starts a cool-down, during which you can't request another review, and each further unsuccessful review may make the next cool-down longer (Google: warnings and account suspensions). That's why we fix everything first and keep the request short and checkable.
