What it looks at

  • Your returns, shipping, privacy and terms pages: there, linked from your home page, and specific (time limits, costs, who pays return postage). WooCommerce's sample returns page left published is a common trap.
  • Contact details a customer could use to identify and reach your business.
  • Wording that reads as a health, results or price claim.
  • On a few products: the price and stock in your product data compared with what the page shows, plus brand and barcode.
  • Whether Google's crawlers are allowed in, and whether your sitemap lists your products.

It only sees your public pages. Merchant Center's own settings (shipping services, return policy, business details) and your feed are covered in the paid Health Check.

Why it's worth fixing before you ask for a review

While an account is suspended, Google says its products are "disabled from appearing across Google" (Google: Issues in Merchant Center). And after a limited number of review requests, Google starts a cool-down during which you can't ask again; each further unsuccessful review may make the next one longer (Google: warnings and account suspensions). Fix everything first, then send one short, checkable request.
