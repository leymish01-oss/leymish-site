Your options

Feeds, catalogs, or later

You can submit product feeds, list through a third-party catalog (Shopify even sells an "Agentic Plan" for non-Shopify brands), or wait for WooCommerce. Every route needs the same basics.

Those basics are what AI agents check before they recommend a product: identifiers they can match (GTIN, brand, MPN), pages they're allowed to fetch, structured data they can trust, and content they can read without running JavaScript. The plugin checks all of it on your own store.

What the audit checks

40 points

Product data

Valid GTIN (check digit verified) or MPN, brand, price, stock, image, a real description, attributes, and complete variations. Per product, with a CSV of every gap.

12 points

AI crawler rules

robots.txt for the search and user bots of OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity, plus Google-Extended: nine user agents in all.

12 points

Real blocks

Requests a product page as each AI crawler and compares it with a normal browser, to catch security plugins and CDN or firewall rules.

12 points

Structured data

Product JSON-LD on your real product pages: price, currency, availability, identifier and brand.

5 points

Readable without JavaScript

Most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript. The product name and price must be in the page itself.

5 points

Store is live

Not stuck in WooCommerce's "coming soon" mode, and not set to discourage search engines. Either hides every product.

14 points

Commerce endpoints

Store API reachable, guest checkout on, llms.txt present, and a UCP profile at /.well-known/ucp.

Questions

Will this get my products into ChatGPT?

No plugin can promise that; OpenAI, Google and others decide what they show. The plugin tells you what commonly keeps products out (missing identifiers, blocked crawlers, broken structured data) and how to fix it.

Does it send my store data anywhere?

The audit only requests your own pages, such as robots.txt and a few product pages. Optional LeyMish services (the weekly tip, AI visibility, AI fixes, Store Team) send only what each one says, and only after you tick a box or click; the plugin's readme lists every one under External services.

Do I need Shopify's Agentic Plan?

No. It's one route among several. Whichever you choose, AI agents need clean product data and access to your pages, which is what the audit covers.

What about WooCommerce MCP?

WooCommerce 10.3 added MCP so AI assistants can help you manage your store. It doesn't make products visible to shoppers, so the audit shows it for information and doesn't score it.

Will it slow my store down?

No. The audit runs only when you click the button in your admin. The feeds rebuild in the background a minute after you save a product.
