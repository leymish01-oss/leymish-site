Set it up in about a minute (Gmail)

  1. Add the forwarding address. Gmail → Settings (gear) → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Add a forwarding address → paste your Reply Desk address. Gmail sends a confirmation code to it; we email you that code within a minute. Enter it in Gmail. (Leave "Disable forwarding" selected there: the filter below forwards only review emails.)
  2. Create a filter. In the Gmail search box, open the filter options. From: google.com OR facebookmail.com OR instagram.com. Has the words: review OR comment OR recommends. Create filter → Forward it to → your Reply Desk address.
  3. Turn on notifications. Google Business Profile → Settings → Notifications → Customer reviews. Facebook Page → Settings → Notifications.
  4. Test it. Forward any old review email to your Reply Desk address yourself. A draft comes back within a minute.

Outlook or another email app? Make a rule that forwards emails from those senders to your Reply Desk address. Stuck? Ask us.

Review-request kit (free)

Ask every customer the same way, with nothing offered in return. That's what the ACCC expects, and it keeps your reviews recent. Paste your review link: on Google Business Profile, open "Ask for reviews" (or "Get more reviews") and copy the link.

Clinics: Ahpra's rules make asking patients for reviews risky, so the kit isn't for clinics.

Questions

Do I need to give you my password?

No. You forward notification emails; we never log in to your Google, Facebook or email account, and we never post for you.

Who writes the drafts?

AI writes each draft from the notification you forwarded, within the rules above. Nothing goes public until you paste and post it.

What do you keep?

Your business name, email and settings. The review text is used to write the draft and email it to you, and isn't stored. Drafts are kept for 30 days so the copy button works. Emails that aren't review or comment notifications are ignored. Privacy.

Can it post replies automatically?

Not yet. That needs Google's and Meta's API approval and your recorded opt-in. For now, one tap copies and one tap opens the review.

What happens after the trial?

Drafts stop unless you subscribe. Nothing is charged automatically.
