What we do

  1. Find the cause. We check your profile against Google's guidelines for representing your business: the business name, address or service area, category, hours, website, photos and recent edits. Most suspensions come from one of a few known triggers.
  2. Fix what's wrong first. A list of the exact changes to make on the profile and on your website, so the appeal shows a business that follows the rules.
  3. Gather the evidence. A checklist tailored to your case, for example a business licence or ABN record, a utility bill or lease in the business name, photos of your signage and premises, or a short video for verification.
  4. Prepare the appeal. The reinstatement or verification request, written for your case, with the evidence attached in the format Google asks for. You send it from your own account (or we do, if you add us as a manager).
  5. Follow up. If Google asks for more, we prepare the reply. Included for 30 days.

Questions

Can you guarantee my profile comes back?
No. Google decides every reinstatement and verification, and no one can honestly promise the outcome. What you get is the work done properly: the cause found, the evidence Google asks for gathered, and a clean appeal.
How long does it take?
We start within one business day of your order and send you the evidence checklist the same day. Google's own review usually takes from a few days to a few weeks after the appeal is sent.
Do you need my Google password?
No. You stay the owner. We guide you through each step, or you add us as a manager on the profile if you prefer; you can remove us afterwards.
What if my profile isn't suspended but stuck in verification?
That's covered too: video verification, a rejected verification or a profile that never went live. We work out what Google needs and prepare it with you.
Do I get a refund if Google says no?
The fee pays for the work, so it isn't refunded because of Google's decision. If we can't do the work described here (for example, the profile was removed for a reason that can't be appealed), we tell you before we start and refund you in full.
