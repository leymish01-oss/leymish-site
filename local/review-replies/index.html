How it works

  1. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile. You stay the owner and can remove us any time. We never ask for your password.
  2. We draft replies to your backlog, up to 100 reviews, in your voice: thanks for the good ones, a calm, specific answer for the bad ones.
  3. You approve them by email, all at once or one by one. Change any word you like.
  4. Then every new review gets a draft within 24 hours. Once you approve it, we post it.

What we never do

  • Write fake reviews, or reviews of your business from anyone connected to it.
  • Offer customers anything in return for a review, or only ask the happy ones. The ACCC's rules forbid both, so the kit asks everyone the same way.
  • Post a reply you haven't approved.
  • Promise a star rating. Google and your customers decide that.

Questions

Who writes the replies?

LeyMish Labs' AI agents write first drafts, with professional oversight, and nothing goes up until you approve it. How we work.

What about a really bad review?

We draft a short, calm reply that takes it offline and flag it to you first. If a review breaks Google's rules, we tell you how to report it; whether it comes down is Google's call.

I run a clinic. Is this allowed?

Replying to reviews is fine. Your replies must not confirm that someone is a patient or discuss their care, and you can't quote patients' reviews in your advertising. Our clinic version follows that, and doesn't ask patients for reviews.

Can I stop?

Yes. Cancel the monthly part any time and remove us as a manager on your profile.
