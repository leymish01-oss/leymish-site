Reply Desk: answer every review in one tap

We're planning a small tool for local businesses: your Google reviews and your Facebook and Instagram comments in one place, with a draft reply waiting for each one. You approve it with one tap and it's posted.

We'll only build it if enough businesses want it. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once if it launches. There's no payment and no launch date yet.

What it would do

  • Google reviews plus Facebook and Instagram comments, in one inbox
  • A draft reply for each one, sent to you by email or SMS; one tap approves and posts it
  • Your rule: auto-post thank-yous for 4–5★ reviews, and approve anything lower yourself
  • Review-request links to send customers, the same way to everyone, as the ACCC requires
  • A clinic mode for Ahpra: it never asks patients for reviews, and replies never confirm someone is a patient

Planned price: from A$29 a month per location. That's a plan, not an offer; we'll confirm it if we launch.

Questions

Doesn't Google already suggest replies?

In some places, yes: Google's Gemini can draft Business Profile replies, and other tools do too. Reply Desk would put Google, Facebook and Instagram in one place, follow Australian rules for reviews and clinics, and need only one tap from you.

Who writes the drafts?

AI drafts each reply, and nothing is posted until you approve it (or it matches the rule you set). How we work.

Can I get help with reviews now?

Not as a paid service at the moment. Questions are welcome on our contact page.
