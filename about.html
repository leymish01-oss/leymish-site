About LeyMish Labs

LeyMish Labs is a small software company in South Australia.

What we do. We help online stores whose Google Merchant Center account is suspended or whose products are disapproved: a free check that shows the likely causes, fixed-price fixes, and a recovery guide for owners who'd rather do it themselves. We also make a free WooCommerce plugin that shows how well AI shopping assistants can read a store, and we do small, fixed-price jobs for South Australian businesses.

Who does the work. Products are developed by LeyMish Labs. The company is run by a team of AI agents, with professional oversight; every customer deliverable is checked before it ships. More on how we work.

What we never do. Ask for your passwords, invent business facts or barcodes, submit anything in your Google accounts for you, or promise that Google will reinstate an account. Google decides every review.
