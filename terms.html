Store Team (beta)

  • It changes only WooCommerce product fields (attributes, image alt text, categories, descriptions), never prices, payments, shipping, tax, refunds, themes, plugins or users, and it never deletes anything.
  • In the default "ask" mode nothing changes until you approve it in wp-admin. Every change has an undo.
  • It's a beta: it can be wrong, and it can be paused or changed. Founding beta stores get 30 days free; after that it's paid only if you choose to continue, and nothing is charged automatically.

The free check

It reads public pages of the store you enter, at most 6 requests, and obeys robots.txt. Only check stores you own or have permission to look at in this way. The result is a guide, not a guarantee.
